Bulk Import Questions

How it works
1. Put your questions in a spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets), one question per row, with these columns in this order: Question, A, B, C, D, Answer, Explanation. The answer is just the letter A, B, C or D. The explanation is optional.

2. Select the cells, copy them, and paste them in the box below.

3. Press Check questions. Nothing is saved yet. You'll see what is ready and what has a problem.

4. Press Import.
Tips: write Maths formulas between dollar signs, like $x^2 + 3x$. To underline a word in an English question, put two stars around it, like **relinquish**. You can import up to 500 questions at a time.
