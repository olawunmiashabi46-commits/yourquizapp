Guidelines for good lessons
Videos
  • Teach one topic per video, following the JAMB syllabus for your subject.
  • Make sure your voice is clear and the writing on the board or screen is easy to read.
  • Work through examples step by step so students can follow your method.
  • Use only your own material. Avoid copyrighted music, images or clips.
  • Give each lesson a clear title and put it under the right subject and topic.
  • Set the video to Public or Unlisted on YouTube so the admin can open it.
Answering students
  • Answer politely and explain how you got the answer, not only what it is.
  • Check Student Questions regularly so students are not left waiting.
Quiz questions
  • Write original questions in the style of the real exam. Do not copy past papers or textbooks.
  • Test understanding, not only memory. Good wrong options are based on mistakes students really make.
  • Give exactly one correct answer and three believable wrong ones.
  • Choose the right subject, topic and difficulty, and add a short explanation.
  • The admin reviews every question. Only approved ones reach students.
